# Case Study Walkthrough

## Business problem
A retail organization wants to understand revenue changes, anticipate near-term performance, detect unusual KPI behavior, and test interventions before committing operational resources.

## Problem framing
DecisionOS separates descriptive, predictive and prescriptive work:

1. **Descriptive:** What changed?
2. **Diagnostic:** Where and why did it change?
3. **Predictive:** What is likely next?
4. **Prescriptive:** What could we change and what is the estimated effect?
5. **Agentic:** How can the workflow execute these analytical steps consistently and produce an auditable decision?

## Evidence chain
Every workflow records the agent, action and structured payload in an audit trace. The LLM layer is intentionally optional; analytical facts are generated by deterministic code first.
